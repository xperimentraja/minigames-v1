LUDO

2 to 4 players use the classic square board, 5 or 6 players use the hexagon board, 7 or 8 players the octagon board. Humans take turns on this screen, the rest are played by the computer. Partners cannot capture each other and can share a cell. A team wins when both partners get all 4 tokens home.
Roll a 6 to leave home. A 6 or a capture gives another turn. Stars and colored start cells are safe. You need the exact roll to reach the center.
